Finished with a ceramic sealant for lasting shine and protection against UV, water spots, and contaminants, plus tire and trim dressing to keep rubber and plastics looking fresh.

Leather Conditioner & Protectant
Interior Care
Restores moisture to dried-out leather while adding a protective barrier against cracking, fading, and everyday wear.

Non-acid foaming cleaner that dissolves brake dust, road grime, and iron fallout from rims, barrels, and tires. Built-in corrosion inhibitors keep bare metal safe.

Pro-grade all-purpose cleaner that cuts through grime on plastics, vinyl, leather, cloth, and headliners without leaving residue or a chemical smell.
Deep clean on every interior surface.
P&S Double Black Carpet Bomber
Carpet & Upholstery
Heavy-duty fabric and carpet cleaner that lifts out stains, odors, and ground-in dirt from seats, mats, and trunk liners.
Brings fabric and carpet back to factory-fresh.
Koch Chemie Protector Wax
Premium Sealant
German-engineered sealant wax that delivers a deep, wet gloss and months of hydrophobic protection against water spots, UV rays, and contaminants.
Showroom shine and lasting protection.

Breaks down grime that other cleaners leave behind.

German-made cleaner and care product for rubber, plastic, and vinyl surfaces inside the cabin. Gently lifts grime from door seals, dashboards, and trim while restoring a clean, factory-matte finish without a greasy feel.
Revives interior rubber and plastics to a like-new look.
ANALAN Pet Hair Remover Blades
Pet Hair Removal
Sharp replacement blades for our pet hair tool. Pulls stubborn pet hair out of fabric, carpets, and upholstery that vacuums and brushes leave behind.
Finally removes embedded pet hair at the root.
